POINTYour arms continuously set your flight direction
ONE ARMReach farther to go from slow to medium speed
TWO ARMSReach farther to go from slow to bullet-fast
HANDS DOWNStop flying
LOOKLook around freely while your arms steer
BANKTilt head right, right hand lower / left higher; reverse for left
LEVELRelax the tilt to smoothly return upright; banking never becomes a barrel roll

Raise your arms a little to drift slowly. Raise and extend farther to accelerate. Rest both at your sides to hover.
